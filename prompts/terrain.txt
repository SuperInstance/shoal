TERRAIN RECIPE (a real Alaska-style bank, west shallow -> east deep):
1. Base plane: depth rises smoothly from ~30 fm at x=0 to ~120 fm at x=99 (smoothstep), plus gentle N->S tilt of +10 fm.
2. Shelf: broad plateau lifting the west third (x < 38) by -12 fm, with value-noise texture so the 40-60 fm band forms a real west face.
3. Basin: gaussian depression, center in SE quadrant chosen by rng (cx in 62..88, cy in 55..85), amplitude +85 fm, sigma ~14 tiles. Deepest tile should land >= 165 fm.
4. Pinnacle: sharp gaussian BUMP (depth decreases) at rng point with x in 35..55, y in 25..60, amplitude -70 fm, sigma ~2.2 tiles. Its peak must be <= 16 fm. Clamp final depths to [6, 235].
5. high2: second smaller bump, amplitude -35 fm, sigma ~5, placed rng in x 20..38, y 55..80.
6. Saddle: after building, find the local minimum-depth... no wait, saddle = the shallowest point on the ridge line BETWEEN pinnacle and high2: sample the straight line between them, saddle = point of maximum depth along that line (the dip between two highs). Store it.
7. Channel: pick xstart in 70..85 at y=0, then walk south y=0..99 in steps of 6, x meanders by rng walk (clamp 62..92), waypoint per step. Along the channel (distance to nearest segment < 3.5 tiles), deepen by +24 fm * falloff.
8. Value noise: 3 octaves of smooth interpolated noise (write your own valueNoise(x,y,scale,seedOffset) with bilinear+smoothstep interpolation of hashed lattice values), total amplitude ~+/-7 fm, applied everywhere for texture.
